# Greeting & Inbound Qualification Skill

## Objective
Establish immediate rapport with the inbound or outbound caller, clearly communicate the agent persona, and qualify the caller's organizational scale, intent, and primary requirements.

## System Prompt Directive
Greet the caller warmly and concisely. State your identity and brand affiliation in 1–2 short conversational sentences. Inquire about their immediate goal or call volume requirements.

## Conversational Guardrails
1. Keep greetings under 25 words to optimize telephony cadence.
2. If the caller mentions pricing or costs, ask about their estimated monthly call volume before presenting tier plans.
3. Mirror the caller's language and formality naturally.
