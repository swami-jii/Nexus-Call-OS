DENTAL CARE & WELLNESS CLINIC — STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES (SOP) & FAQs
===================================================================

1. CLINIC OPERATING HOURS & LOCATION
------------------------------------
- Clinic Address: 104 Medical Enclave, Sector 18, City Center.
- Business Hours: Monday to Saturday (9:00 AM – 8:00 PM IST).
- Closed: Sundays and National Holidays.
- 24/7 Emergency Receptionist Line: +91 (800) 555-0199.

2. APPOINTMENT BOOKING & CANCELLATION POLICY
--------------------------------------------
- Booking Advance: Appointments must be booked at least 2 hours in advance.
- Cancellation Fee: Free cancellation up to 24 hours prior to appointment time.
- Late Cancellation Fee (Less than 24h notice): ₹500 cancellation fee applies.
- No-Show Penalty: ₹1,000 penalty deducted on next visit if patient fails to inform.
- Rescheduling: Up to 2 free reschedules allowed per appointment booking.

3. TREATMENT PRICING & CONSULTATION FEES
----------------------------------------
- General Dental Consultation Fee: ₹500 per visit.
- Professional Teeth Cleaning & Scaling: ₹1,500 – ₹2,500.
- Root Canal Treatment (RCT): ₹4,500 per tooth (includes digital X-ray).
- Zirconia Dental Crown: ₹8,000 per crown (with 10-year warranty).
- Teeth Whitening (Laser Bleaching): ₹6,000 per session.
- Dental Implant (Single Tooth): ₹25,000 – ₹35,000.

4. INSURANCE & PAYMENT MODES ACCEPTED
-------------------------------------
- Payment Modes: UPI, Google Pay, Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, Cash, & EMI.
- Insurance Coverage: Direct cashless insurance claims processed for Star Health, HDFC ERGO, Max Bupa, Care Health, and ICICI Lombard.
- Co-Pay Requirement: Standard 10% co-pay applicable at check-in for cashless claims.

5. EMERGENCY & POST-TREATMENT CARE SOP
--------------------------------------
- Severe Dental Toothache / Trauma: Immediate priority walk-in accepted between 9 AM and 8 PM.
- Post-Op Care Rule: Avoid hot foods/beverages for 4 hours after root canal or extraction.
