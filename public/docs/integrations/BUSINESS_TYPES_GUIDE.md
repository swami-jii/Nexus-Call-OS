# Business Vertical Types & Industry Profiles — Operational Guide

Business Types define **Industry Vertical Guidelines, Terminology, and Guardrails** tailored specifically for your organization (Dental Clinic, Real Estate, Debt Collection, E-Commerce, Legal Advisory).

---

## 🎯 Role in Live Phone Calls & Purpose
Different industries require different greeting phrases, compliance rules, and professional vocabulary. Setting a Business Type configures your AI Voice Agents to automatically speak using appropriate industry terminology and compliance standards.

---

## 📌 Kahan Use Hoga? (Where Is This Used?)
- Used when onboarding new client accounts, setting up industry-specific voice agents, and enforcing sector regulations (HIPAA for healthcare, TCPA for sales outreach).

---

## 💡 Real-World Voice Calling Example
- **Scenario**: Setting up a Dental Clinic AI receptionist.
- **Execution**: Select **Dental & Healthcare Clinic** business profile. The AI Agent automatically adopts a empathetic, polite medical tone, greeting patients with:
  > *"Thank you for calling Dental Care Clinic! How can I assist you with your appointment or dental inquiry today?"*

---

## 🚀 Project me Kyu Zaroori hai? (Why Is This Critical?)
1. **Industry-Specific Intelligence**: AI Agents sound professional and knowledgeable in their specific field.
2. **Built-in Compliance**: Enforces industry guardrails out of the box.
3. **Custom Business Behaviors**: Add custom business rules if your domain requires special handling.

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Setup Instructions
1. **Select Industry Vertical**: Choose Healthcare, Sales SDR, Real Estate, E-Commerce, or Custom.
2. **Configure Greeting & Behavior**: Input initial greeting phrase and primary business behavior.
3. **Set Default Operating Hours**: Link default working hours and timezone schedules.
