# Languages, Locales & Code-Switching — Operational Guide

Languages & Locales manage **Multilingual Speech Recognition, Voice Synthesis, and Real-Time Code-Switching** (e.g. Hinglish, Spanglish).

---

## 🎯 Role in Live Phone Calls & Purpose
Callers frequently switch between languages during phone calls (e.g. speaking Hindi mixed with English words). Language configuration enables AI Agents to recognize mixed speech naturally and respond in the caller's preferred language.

---

## 📌 Kahan Use Hoga? (Where Is This Used?)
- Used across global customer support, regional sales campaigns in India, US Hispanic markets, and international voice helplines.

---

## 💡 Real-World Voice Calling Example
- **Scenario**: Caller in Delhi says: *"Haal hi me maine appointment book kiya tha, kya main cancellation charges ke baare me jaan sakta hu?"*
- **Execution**: The AI Agent detects Hinglish (Hindi + English), transcribes both languages seamlessly, and responds in natural fluent Hinglish voice.

---

## 🚀 Project me Kyu Zaroori hai? (Why Is This Critical?)
1. **Natural Multilingual Calls**: Eliminates language barriers for regional callers.
2. **Auto Language Detection**: AI Agent automatically detects caller language within the first 3 seconds.
3. **Regional Accent Alignment**: Matches STT/TTS models to local accents (English US/UK/IN, Hindi, Spanish, French).

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Setup Instructions
1. **Primary Language**: Select primary workspace language (e.g. English US, Hindi, Spanish).
2. **Enable Multilingual Auto-Detect**: Turn on real-time language detection.
3. **Configure Currency & Date Format**: Set regional number, date, and currency formatting.
