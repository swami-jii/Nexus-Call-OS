# Vision & Document AI Models — Operational Guide

Vision & Document AI models enable **Structural Document Extraction (OCR) and Image Understanding** for processing complex documents and visual files.

---

## 🎯 Role in Live Phone Calls & Purpose
When customers submit complex scanned PDFs, medical insurance cards, identity documents, or bill receipts, Vision & Document AI parses structural data into text and JSON for AI Agents.

---

## 📌 Kahan Use Hoga? (Where Is This Used?)
- Used for extracting table data from scanned PDF uploads, OCR receipt processing, and visual document analysis.

---

## 💡 Real-World Voice Calling Example
- **Scenario**: A patient uploads a scanned insurance claim document before an AI appointment call.
- **Execution**: Azure Document Intelligence / GPT-4o Vision extracts policy numbers, claim amounts, and table fields in 300ms, making the data instantly available to the AI Receptionist.

---

## 🚀 Project me Kyu Zaroori hai? (Why Is This Critical?)
1. **Automated Document Extraction**: No manual data entry from scanned PDFs or photos.
2. **Multimodal Capability**: Processes images, screenshots, and structural table data seamlessly.
3. **Cloud & Local OCR Support**: Supports Azure Doc Intelligence, Unstructured.io, GPT-4o Vision, and Local OCR.

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Setup Instructions
1. **Select Provider**: Choose Azure Doc Intelligence, GPT-4o Vision, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, or Local OCR.
2. **Input API Key**: Enter API Secret key.
3. **Enable Capabilities**: Check Structural PDF OCR and Image Understanding toggles.
