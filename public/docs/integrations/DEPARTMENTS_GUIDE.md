# Departments & Warm Call Transfer Routing — Operational Guide

Departments govern **Team Extension Routing, Escalation Queues, and Warm Hand-Offs** to human staff.

---

## 🎯 Role in Live Phone Calls & Purpose
When a caller on an AI phone call requests to speak with a human specialist (e.g. *"Transfer me to Billing"* or *"I need a senior dentist"*), Department rules route the live call to the correct extension or phone queue.

---

## 📌 Kahan Use Hoga? (Where Is This Used?)
- Used during live calls when AI Agents perform warm transfers, round-robin agent dispatch, or manager escalation.

---

## 💡 Real-World Voice Calling Example
- **Scenario**: A patient calls with a complex insurance dispute.
- **Execution**: The AI Agent detects high complexity, says *"Hold on a moment, transferring you to our Billing Manager"*, and performs a **Warm Hand-off**—sending the call transcript to the manager's screen before bridging the audio line.

---

## 🚀 Project me Kyu Zaroori hai? (Why Is This Critical?)
1. **Seamless Human-AI Hybrid Operations**: Human staff step in effortlessly when calls require human judgment.
2. **Warm Transcript Hand-off**: Human staff read call summary notes before answering so callers don't repeat themselves.
3. **Smart Load Balancing**: Supports Round-Robin, Ring All, and Priority Escalation strategies.

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Setup Instructions
1. **Department Name & Extension**: Enter department title and extension number (e.g. `#102 Billing`).
2. **Transfer Strategy**: Select Round-Robin, Ring All, Manager Escalation, or Custom.
3. **Manager Contact**: Assign department lead email or phone number for notifications.
