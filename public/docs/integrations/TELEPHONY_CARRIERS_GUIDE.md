# Telephony Carriers Configuration & Setup Guide — Operational Guide

Telephony Carriers connect your AI Voice Agents directly to **PSTN Telephone Networks Worldwide**.

---

## 🎯 Role in Live Phone Calls & Purpose
Carriers provide phone numbers (DIDs) and voice trunks required to place outbound automated campaign calls and receive inbound customer calls.

---

## 📌 Kahan Use Hoga? (Where Is This Used?)
- Used across all phone calling activities in Nexus Call OS.

---

## 💡 Real-World Voice Calling Example
- **Scenario**: Dialing 5,000 outbound leads across North America and India.
- **Execution**: Telephony Carrier (Twilio, Telnyx, Plivo) connects the call to local PSTN phone networks in <300ms.

---

## 🚀 Project me Kyu Zaroori hai? (Why Is This Critical?)
1. **Global Reach**: Connect to phone networks in 100+ countries.
2. **High Delivery Rate**: Enterprise carriers ensure clean caller ID presentation.
3. **SSOT Credential Masking**: Telephony credentials are encrypted and stored safely.

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Setup Instructions
1. **Select Carrier**: Choose Twilio, Telnyx, Plivo, Vonage, or Custom Voice API.
2. **Input API SID & Secret**: Enter Account SID / API Key and Auth Secret.
3. **Test Connection**: Click `Test Connection` to verify carrier API responsiveness.
