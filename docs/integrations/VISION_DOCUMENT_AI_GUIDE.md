# Nexus Call OS - Vision & Document AI Integration Guide

Vision & Document AI models process complex multimodal inputs (PDF forms, structural tables, images, UI screenshots) for AI workflows.

## 🎯 Role in Live Phone Calls & Workflows
1. Parses PDF forms, invoices, and contracts uploaded by callers or agents.
2. Extracts tabular data, visual bounding boxes, and image descriptions.
3. Feeds structured JSON data into workflow automation tools and CRM records.

## 🛠️ Key Capabilities
- **Structural PDF OCR**: Extracts multi-column tables and text hierarchy into Markdown.
- **Image Understanding**: Analyzes visual photos and diagram structures.
